FAQ

Questions
answered

Can't find what you're looking for? Chat with our team — we usually reply within a few hours.

How does the consultation work?▾
Your consultation takes place over video call or in-person (in select cities). Your curator will guide you through a series of questions about your home, lifestyle, scent history, and goals. No preparation needed — just show up curious.
Do I need to buy Flamora products?▾
The Essentials plan provides recommendations you can fulfil from any source. The Signature and Annual plans include a hand-curated kit from our botanical range — but we'll always tell you exactly what to look for if you prefer to source independently.
How long before I receive my kit?▾
After your consultation and sign-off on the scent map, kits are dispatched within 2 business days and arrive in 3–5 days across India. Express delivery is available at checkout.
Can I gift this to someone?▾
Absolutely. Fragrance curation makes an extraordinary gift. Select a plan, add a personalised note at checkout, and we'll send the recipient a beautifully packaged voucher card with instructions to book their consultation.
What if I'm not happy with the result?▾
We offer one free revision session within 30 days of delivery. If after the revision you're still not satisfied, we'll refund or exchange products — no awkward questions.
Is this available outside India?▾
The consultation and scent map service is available globally via video call. Physical product delivery currently covers India. International shipping is in beta — contact us to check your location.
